# Réel vs simulé

Anything simulated carries a visible **Simulé** badge in the UI. Money on a test network carries a **Fonds de test** badge, and the footer of every public page says which of the three is in play (simulated payments, Lightning test network, or Bitcoin Lightning).

**Three wallet set-ups:**

- **Simulé** (`WALLET_MODE=fake`): no Lightning at all. FakeWallet invoices are BOLT11-shaped (`lnbcrt`), and their preimages hash to the payment hash.
- **Fonds de test** (`WALLET_MODE=nwc`, `NETWORK_LABEL=test`): real Lightning payments over Nostr Wallet Connect, between test wallets from Alby's NWC faucet (`faucet.nwc.dev`). Their balances "are not real and exist only inside the NWC Faucet". Invoices, preimages, payouts and receipts are all real; the sats have no value.
- **Réel** (`WALLET_MODE=nwc`, `NETWORK_LABEL=mainnet`): a real wallet with real sats.
